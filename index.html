👥 1. Target User Personas

Research evaluated three primary student archetypes representing diverse physical, environmental, and technological constraints:

Asha, 19
Mobile-First Intern
  • Hardware: Entry-level Android, 3G/4G network
  • Context: First technical internship
  • Goal: Submit proof link and confirm receipt
  • Pain: No upload progress indicators; accidental double submits
  • Need: Plain instructions & visible progress
Ravi, 22
Assistive Tech User
  • Hardware: Windows laptop, screen magnifier (200%)
  • Context: Keyboard navigation user
  • Goal: Complete checklist without mouse
  • Pain: Disappearing focus rings; modal focus traps
  • Need: Scalable text & predictable focus
Meera, 20
Time-Constrained Learner
  • Hardware: Shared family tablet / PC
  • Context: 20–30 min study windows
  • Goal: Draft submissions in short sessions
  • Pain: Silent session timeouts wipe out work
  • Need: Auto-saved drafts & session resilience

❓ 2. User Research Survey & Interview Questions

Five targeted questions deployed during discovery to unearth critical usability and cognitive barriers:

1
Comprehension: When you open an assignment brief, what is the first thing you look for, and how easily can you identify passing criteria?
2
Resource Discovery: Have you ever struggled to find starter templates, checklists, or sample datasets? What visual affordance was missing?
3
Submission Reassurance: Describe your experience pasting and submitting a proof link. Did you feel 100% confident your work was safely logged?
4
Feedback Recovery: When an assignment requires revisions or is rejected, does the system explain exactly which checklist items failed?
5
Ergonomic & Assistive Constraints: What device-specific issues (e.g. tiny touch targets, disappearing focus rings, session timeouts) cause you to abandon a session?

Key Findings Across Critical Categories

  • Navigation: Absence of Skip Link (WCAG 2.4.1) forces 18 tab presses. Keyboard focus outline was removed (`outline: none`), failing WCAG 2.4.7.
  • Readability: Reading level measured at Flesch-Kincaid 11.4; needs reduction to 7.0–8.5 for plain inclusive language.
  • Forms: Missing explicit `
  • Mobile Usability (360px): Resource buttons measure 28px height, violating the WCAG 44x44px touch target standard.

🚀 5. Portfolio Upgrade: Prioritization Matrix

Findings prioritized using an Impact vs. Effort framework to guide the upcoming Task 03 wireframing sprint:

P1
Submission Loading & Success State (High Impact / Low Effort): Provide animated loading spinner, disable button on click, and render verifiable confirmation receipt.
P1
Auto-Saved Draft State (High Impact / Med Effort): Implement `localStorage` draft caching to safeguard students on unstable sessions.
P1
Accessible Modal Focus Trap (High Impact / Med Effort): Ensure keyboard focus is trapped inside dialogs and `:focus-visible` outlines are restored.
P2
Accessible Touch Targets & Floating Labels (Med Impact / Low Effort): Enlarge clickable areas to 48x48px and replace disappearing placeholders with persistent labels.
